Changes made (derivative material)
  - Cut a 42-second excerpt (native 148-190 s) from the original recording.
  - Cropped a fixed 1152x648 region at x=768, y=432 of the source frame. This
    is a fixed crop of the same camera, not a different location, and it is
    not a seamless composition.
  - Scaled the crop to 960x540 with square pixels.
  - Removed audio and re-encoded as H.264 MP4 (CRF 25, native 30 fps,
    faststart).
  - Extracted a still frame as the poster.
  - tracking.json is original work of this project: hand-authored normalized
    bounds (top-left origin) for two neutral head tracks (subject-01,
    subject-02), measured from the excerpt's own video timestamps. It is not
    output of live inference and not an identification of any person.
