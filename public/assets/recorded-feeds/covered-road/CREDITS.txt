Changes made (derivative material)

    composition.

  - Removed audio and metadata and re-encoded as H.264 MP4 (crf 24, fast preset).

  - tracking.json is original work of this project: hand-authored normalized
    bounds (top-left origin) for one neutral vehicle track, not output of live
    inference and not an identification of any vehicle or person.
