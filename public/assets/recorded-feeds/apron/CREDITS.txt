Changes made (derivative material)
  - Cut a 33-second forward-time segment. An earlier cut began 2 seconds
    sooner and opened with a rapid zoom-out; those 2 seconds were removed so
    the camera stays fixed under the marker, and the clip was re-encoded.
  - Silent H.264 (yuv420p), 960x540 at 24 fps; audio removed.
  - Extracted a new still frame at t=0 as the poster.
  - tracking.json: hand-authored whole-object bounds (normalized, top-left
    origin) sampled from the final encoded frames. The keys were resampled
    from the earlier cut and shifted by -2 seconds to match this one. Not
    output of live inference and not an identification of anyone or anything.

Notes
  - This is an ordinary recorded replay shown inside a fictional interface.
    It is not a live camera. The aircraft is parked, not flying. A wide
    stationary apron shot; slight framing drift remains. Distant ground
    personnel are not marked (too small for reliable bounds at this
    encoding). The clip restarts at its end and the loop point is not
    certified seamless.
  - This material is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or the
    videographer.
