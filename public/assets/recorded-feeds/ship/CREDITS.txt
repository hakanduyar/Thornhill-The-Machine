Changes made (derivative material)
  - Cut seconds 22-54 (32 seconds) from the source file.
  - Resized to 720x540 with square pixels, then pillarboxed to 960x540 with
    120-pixel black side bars.
  - Re-encoded as H.264 MP4; audio removed.
  - Extracted a still frame as the poster.
  - tracking.json: hand-authored whole-vessel bounds (normalized, top-left
    origin, adjusted for the side bars) measured on the final encoded frames
    at 2-second intervals; the distant second vessel is left untracked after
    26 seconds. Not output of live inference and not an identification of
    anyone or anything.

Notes
  - This is an ordinary recorded replay shown inside a fictional interface.
    It is not a live camera. The camera is fixed; the clip starts with the
    ship docked and ends after it has left, so the loop point is not
    seamless and shows a visible restart.
  - This material is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or the
    videographers.
